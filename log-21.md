THE FRICTION POINT.

The Unfiltered Realist

Meeting an earning platform’s threshold is one thing.

Moving the resulting value through the next system is another.

Each step can work by its own rules, and the journey can still meet a constraint between them.

LOG 020 recorded the route. Some links between its steps were unverified.

This log goes back to the records. Not to erase the journey, but to separate what happened, what the evidence establishes, and what we should have captured along the way.

The claim: the 3 September deposit is close to the redeemed amount, not the same.

What this comparison adds: the route has worked. The September withdrawal, at about $4.10, was below the reported $5 P2P minimum.

MODEL CHECK.

The Good, the Bad, the Ugly

Good: the exchange’s records tie Link 3 exactly. The platform now publishes its partner, rate and fee.

Bad: three links rest on captures we did not take. The transfer is undated. One observer, one coder.

Ugly: A fee with a fixed component takes a larger share of a small payout.

$1.00 = 13.5%

$2.33 = approximately 6.7%

$5.00 = 3.9%

These are calculations from the 27 October fee schedule, not observed charges from the August redemption.

Method lesson: when evidence is captured after the event, connections get harder to rebuild. Record each transition as it happens: the quote and its fee, the amounts in and out, the date, the source and the destination.

The Two Frictions

Route friction. The amount at one stage may not meet the conditions of the next. The September withdrawal, about $4.10, was below the reported $5 P2P minimum. A published fixed fee takes a larger share of a small amount.

Evidence friction. When transitions are not recorded as they happen, later reconstruction cannot always establish continuity, even when the journey is plausible. Three links rest on records we did not capture.

Neither invalidates the pilot. Both are what the pilot produced.

Working thesis (candidate, this route only): meeting an earning threshold is one achievement. Moving the resulting value through successive systems is another. The links reveal the conditions between the two.

What this pilot does not establish: that the 3 September deposit is the redemption, where the withdrawal went, what fee was actually charged, or how often any of this happens to other operators, platforms or routes.

Boundary: one observed route can reveal a mechanism worth investigating. It cannot show how often it occurs.

## What This Is

This is not a blog archive.

This is a field intelligence repository — a structured record of one operator's navigation through the digital economy from the edge of the network, not the centre of it.

Every log entry is written from **North Kinangop, Kenya**, filed from a setup that runs on fluctuating grid power, mobile data, and systems designed by people who have never had to buy a data bundle before submitting a form.

The dispatches are raw. The math is real. The analysis is built from what actually happens when global platforms meet local infrastructure — not what the pitch deck says should happen.

---

## The Core Thesis

The internet created a highway.  
Highways need bridges.  
AI models, remote work platforms, and digital marketplaces are the highway.  
Payment systems, digital identity, financial access, and last-mile infrastructure are the bridges.

**Everyone talks about the destination. Few study the roads.**

This archive studies the roads.

## The Operating Principles

**Rule #1:** Never let a single platform hold more than your next data bundle.

**Rule #2:** Accumulate to dilute. Do not withdraw at minimum threshold when flat fees eat your capital.

**Rule #3:** The platform isn't malicious. It's blind to the last mile. Your job is to see what it cannot.

**Rule #4:** Understand the plumbing, or get washed away by the fees.

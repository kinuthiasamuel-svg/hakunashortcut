## PART 07 — SIGNAL RULES

**Rule S-01:** Log the signal within 72 hours of transmission.
Patterns decay. Fresh signal is accurate signal.

**Rule S-02:** Distinguish between noise and signal.
One comment is noise. Three comments on the same point is signal.
Five is a pattern. A pattern changes the roadmap.

**Rule S-03:** Friction is intelligence, not failure.
When a reader pushes back — log it precisely.
The friction point is where the next LOG lives.

**Rule S-04:** The Signal Log feeds the roadmap.
Any Intelligence Gap that appears in 3+ transmissions
gets promoted to the LOG_ROADMAP.md as a planned entry.

**Rule S-05:** Never optimize for the signal alone.
The Captain files what is true, not what performs.
Signal informs. It does not command.
